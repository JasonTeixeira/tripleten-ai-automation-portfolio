**Sprint:** AI Tools and Prompt Engineering
**Scenario:** Hotel assistant
**Built with:** Zapier Chatbots

## Purpose
A conversational assistant that answers common guest questions — check-in/check-out
times, amenities, Wi-Fi, parking, and booking modifications — grounded strictly in the
uploaded Triple Peaks Hotel FAQ. It keeps a professional hospitality tone and falls back
to front-desk contact details for anything outside its knowledge base.
