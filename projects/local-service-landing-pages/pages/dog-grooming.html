Questions, answered

How do I book?

Hit any "Book" button, pick your dog's size and a time — you'll get an instant confirmation. No account needed.

Do you use cages?

Never. We groom one dog at a time in an open, calm room and use towel-and-air drying for anxious pups.

What if my dog is nervous or senior?

Tell us in the booking notes. We slow the whole visit down and can split it across two shorter appointments.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free to reschedule up to 24 hours before. Life happens with pets — just let us know.
