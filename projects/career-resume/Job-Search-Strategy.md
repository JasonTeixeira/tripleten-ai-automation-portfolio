# Jason Teixeira Job Search Strategy

I am targeting roles where I can combine software development, finance experience and AI automation to improve recurring business processes. This plan guides my applications, portfolio updates and career-coach discussions.

## Target roles and location

My primary targets are AI Automation Specialist, Junior Automation Developer and Workflow Automation Engineer. Related targets include QA Engineer, Technical Support Specialist and early-career software roles where my project evidence fits the requirements. I am based in Orlando, Florida and am focusing on US-remote roles and relevant local opportunities.

## Value proposition

I combine computer science and finance training with independent business-software development and remote fintech work. I build workflows that connect services, validate AI-generated outputs and preserve useful evidence when something fails. I can explain the workflow and its limitations clearly to a reviewer or teammate.

## Relevant skills and proof

- n8n, Make and Zapier for service integration and workflow routing.

- Python, REST APIs, JSON, JSON Schema and SQLite for structured processing and validation.

- LLM prompts, output checks, exception handling, automated testing and technical documentation.

- Supplier Delay Triage: an 11-node n8n workflow with source checks, review routing and duplicate prevention; 42 passing tests and 94% ledger coverage.

- Customer Feedback Analysis: seven tested feedback inputs and verified alerts for two negative responses.

- Hotel FAQ Assistant: five FAQ examples, an out-of-scope fallback and live verification.

## Employers and industries

I prioritize SaaS, fintech, business services and healthcare technology employers whose products depend on reliable workflows, integrations or customer-facing software. For each role I check location eligibility, required skills and the actual employer posting before investing in an application.

## Application and networking plan

My weekly goal is 10 carefully selected, tailored applications. I will adjust that target with my career coach based on fit and response rates. For each application I will match my summary, skills and project examples to the role, review the exported PDF and record the actual submission status.

I will research relevant recruiters and employees, write short personalized outreach, and record contacts and follow-ups. I will follow up about one week after applying when appropriate and respect employer instructions about contact.

## Weekly routine and improvement

Monday: screen roles and prioritize the shortlist. Tuesday and Wednesday: tailor materials and apply. Thursday: networking and follow-ups. Friday: review tracker results, refine the resume and improve a portfolio example. I will track applications, responses, interviews and follow-up dates in Percruit, and review patterns with my coach.
